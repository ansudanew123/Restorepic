Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my completed restored photo?

We will first send a watermarked digital preview directly to your WhatsApp or Email. Once you inspect and approve the result, you pay via UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), and we instantly send you the high-resolution unwatermarked file.

What if I don't have a scanner for my old photograph?

No scanner needed! Simply take a clear photo of your old physical photo using your smartphone camera in well-lit daylight (avoid heavy glass reflections) and upload it here.

What is the PVC Card Layout service?

If you have downloaded digital PDF documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN card, or Voter ID), we resize and format front-and-back pages precisely into standard CR80 wallet card size so you can get them printed on PVC plastic easily.
