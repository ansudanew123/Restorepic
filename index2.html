Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my completed restored photo?

We send a watermarked digital preview to your WhatsApp or Email first. Once you approve the result, pay via UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) and we immediately send the unwatermarked HD file.

What if I don't have a scanner for my old photograph?

No scanner needed. Take a clear photo of your physical print using your phone camera in bright daylight (avoid glass glare) and send it on WhatsApp.

What is the PVC Card Layout service?

We resize and align your downloaded Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID PDFs (front & back) to exact CR80 wallet-card dimensions, ready for PVC printing.
