Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my completed restored photo?

We send a watermarked digital preview directly to your WhatsApp or Email first. Once you inspect and approve the result, pay via UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) and we immediately send the unwatermarked HD file.

What if I don't have a scanner for my old photograph?

No scanner needed. Place the photo near a bright window (avoiding glass glare), take a clear straight-on photo with your phone camera, and send it on WhatsApp.

What is the PVC Card Layout service?

We resize and align your downloaded Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID PDFs (front & back) into exact CR80 standard wallet-card dimensions so they are ready for clean PVC card printing.

Are my personal documents and family photos kept private?

Yes, 100%. Your photos and ID documents are never shared publicly and are deleted from our working folder after delivery unless you request a backup.
